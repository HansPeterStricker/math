Graphs of mathematics

For each area of mathematics there are two graphs. The concept graph shows the definitions and their use in other definitions: an arrow leads from A to B when B is defined with A. The theorem graph shows the theorems, lemmas and conjectures with their proofs: an arrow leads from each theorem a usual proof uses to the theorem it proves. In both, each MSC class has a sector, and a node lies further out than the nodes it builds on. Concepts and theorems of one area used in another link the graphs of the two areas.
