Graphs of mathematics

For each area of mathematics there will be several graphs: the theorems and their use in proofs, the definitions and their use in other definitions and theorems, and the areas and their relations to each other. Concepts that are defined in one area and used in another link the graphs of the two areas.

MSC 03E, 06A

Set theory

Concept graph: sets, relations and maps, orders, ordinals and cardinals, and the construction of ℕ, ℤ, ℚ, ℝ and ℂ.
MSC 11A

Elementary number theory

Concept graph: divisibility, congruences, arithmetic functions, primes, continued fractions.
MSC 20

Group theory

Concept graph: groups and homomorphisms, group actions and permutations, finite and abelian groups.
MSC 13

Commutative algebra

Concept graph: rings and ideals, localization, modules, chain conditions, special and local rings, completion.
MSC 18

Category theory

Concept graph: categories, functors and limits, abelian categories and homological algebra, sheaves, higher categories.
MSC 14

Algebraic geometry

Concept graph: schemes and morphisms, sheaves and cohomology, divisors and line bundles, curves, surfaces, birational geometry, moduli, abelian varieties.
MSC 11F, 11G, 11M, 11N, 11R, 11S

Arithmetic geometry

Concept graph: L-functions, primes, number fields and class field theory, local fields, modular forms, elliptic curves over number fields.

Theorem graphs, further areas and the graph of the areas will follow.
