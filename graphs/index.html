Graphs of mathematics

For each area of mathematics there are three graphs. The concept graph shows the definitions and their use in other definitions: an arrow leads from A to B when B is defined with A. The theorem graph shows the theorems, lemmas and conjectures with their proofs: an arrow leads from each theorem a usual proof uses to the theorem it proves. In both, each MSC class has a sector, and a node lies further out than the nodes it builds on. The graph of concepts in theorems puts the concepts in a column on the left and the theorems in a column on the right and joins a concept to every theorem whose statement uses it. Concepts and theorems of one area used in another link the graphs of the two areas. The graph of the areas shows these links between all areas and their MSC classes.
