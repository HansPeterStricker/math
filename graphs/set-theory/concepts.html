Concepts of set theory

    About this graph

    Each point is a definition from set theory (MSC 03E, ordered sets 06A), up to the construction of the number systems. An arrow from A to B means that the definition of B uses A. Dashed lines mean “every B is an A”.

    Each MSC class has its own sector of the circle. The distance from the centre shows the level within the area: within a sector a concept lies further out than the concepts its definition uses. The colour shows the MSC class; larger points are used, directly or indirectly, by more definitions.

    Concepts defined in another area are not points of this graph. When a concept whose definition uses one of them is selected, an arrow comes from outside, from a small point with its name in the colour of that area; a click on the point opens the graph of that area with the concept marked. The switch “arrows from other areas” shows all these arrows.

    “Is a” relations and arrows from other areas (prerequisites such as ℕ and ℤ) appear when a concept is selected; indirect dependencies (A → C when C also uses B and B depends on A) and edges used only by other definitions only with their switches on the left.

    A click on a concept lays the card of its definition over the dimmed graph: the concept at the bottom with the concepts its definition uses directly above it (brown). A click on any box of the card shows or hides the boxes above it; a concept with several definitions has a small switch “1/3 ›” for the next one. A right click lays the card of the consequences: the concept at the top, below it the concepts that use it in some definition (blue; dashed where not every definition uses it); a click on a box shows or hides the boxes below it. The card closes with ×, Escape or a click outside it and starts afresh the next time. The small “↗” at the lower left of a box opens the card of that node (for a node of another area its own graph); “‹ Back” returns to the previous card. With the switch “click opens a card” turned off, a click marks instead, in the graph itself, the concepts its definition uses directly, and each further click on it one step more; a right click marks in the same way the concepts that use it. The panel shows the definition and, in the same drawing, all definitions it builds on. While a concept is selected, all levels are marked in the dimmed graph. Arrows to other areas lead to the concepts of other areas whose definitions use it. Pointing at a concept shows its name at once; after half a second only this concept stays lit. Drag a concept to move it, drag the background to move the picture, use the mouse wheel to zoom. The search field finds concepts by name; the concept found is ringed in yellow and the view is centred on it.

    The contents were compiled with the help of a language model and have not been checked by a specialist.
