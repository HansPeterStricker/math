Pairs and triples of areas

Two or three strongly connected areas as touching circles. Inside each circle stand the nodes of that area that are joined with a node of one of the others, near the point where the circles touch; nodes joined with both others stand towards the middle. Squares are concepts, circles theorems, diamonds conjectures. A coloured ring marks a node that also carries an MSC class of another of the areas. A thick outline marks a bridge: a theorem whose statement uses concepts of two of the areas. The numbers where the circles touch count all single edges between the two areas, as in the graph of the areas.
